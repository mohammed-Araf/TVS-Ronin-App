RONIN SOLO TEST - phone only
============================
BEFORE (at home, once):
- Developer options > Enable Bluetooth HCI snoop log = Enabled
- Turn Bluetooth OFF then ON (starts recording)

AT THE BIKE - wait ~10 seconds between every step.

PART A - official TVS app
A1  Ignition ON. Open TVS Connect, let it connect. Do nothing for 30 s.
A2  Press I button ONCE (short). Wait 10 s.
A3  Press I button LONG (hold 2 s). Wait 10 s.
A4  Press I button TWICE fast. Wait 10 s.
A5  Press I button once more (short). Wait 10 s.
A6  Mode button: Urban > Rain > Urban.
A7  Left indicator on/off, right indicator on/off, high beam on/off.
A8  Side stand up, then down.
A9  (optional) Send an SMS to YOUR OWN number from the phone.
A10 Start engine, idle 30 s, rev gently twice, switch engine off.
A11 Clutch in, shift 1st > neutral (engine off).
A12 Force-stop TVS Connect (Settings > Apps > TVS Connect > Force stop).

PART B - nRF Connect (our own messages)
B1  Open nRF Connect > connect TVS22XXXXXXXX > Client > Unknown Service.
B2  Characteristic 0x5354: tap the triple-arrow (subscribe).
B3  Characteristic 0x5352: tap the up-arrow (write) > BYTE ARRAY > paste ONE line below > Send.
    Watch the bike display after each, wait 10 s.

    Caller 9876543210:

    Text HELLO RONIN:

B3b Display brightness: DONE 2026-11-25 (see PROTOCOL.md, 5A F1). Levels 1-10 = 10-100 %.

B4  Press I button ONCE while nRF Connect is connected.
B5  Disconnect. Ignition OFF.

WRITE DOWN what the bike display showed for each B3 line (or take a photo).
Then back home: plug phone into the Mac and tell Claude.
